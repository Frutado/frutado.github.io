Carbon Lamination

A primer on laminating with carbon fiber

What is Carbon Lamination?

Carbon lamination is the process of reinforcing materials by bonding layers of carbon fiber fabric with resin. The resulting composite is lightweight, strong, and resistant to corrosion.

Benefits

  • High strength-to-weight ratio
  • Corrosion resistance
  • Enhanced rigidity
  • Aesthetic carbon fiber weave appearance

Applications

Carbon lamination is used in aerospace, automotive, sports equipment, and custom design projects where performance and weight savings matter.

The Process

  1. Prepare the surface by cleaning and sanding.
  2. Cut carbon fiber fabric to size.
  3. Mix epoxy resin according to manufacturer instructions.
  4. Apply resin and lay fabric, removing air bubbles.
  5. Repeat layers as needed and allow to cure.
